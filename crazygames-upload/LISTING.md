## Title
Ping Palace

## Short description
Run a chaotic first-person net café: seat gamers, fix PCs, sell snacks, punch troublemakers and survive the weirdest shifts in town.

## Description
Ping Palace is a first-person net café simulator where every shift goes sideways. Open the doors at 08:00, seat gamers at your PCs, hand out sodas and snacks, collect the cash and keep the place running until midnight.

Your customers have needs, opinions and very little patience. PCs crash, toilets clog, pigeons fly in, the power goes out and a man in a banana suit sprints laps around the room. Troublemakers try to rob your register and smash your hardware, so punch them out. Every character is a full ragdoll, and a charged MEGA punch sends them flying across the café.

Spend your earnings to grow. Buy new PC stations and carry them into place, upgrade from beige Potato PCs to curved Titan X rigs, add racing chairs, a coffee machine, an arcade cabinet, neon decor, a sound system and a robot janitor, then knock down the wall to open the VIP lounge.

Features:
- First-person café management with a full day and night cycle
- 25 random weird events, including disco fever, a gravity glitch, PC fires, money rain, a pigeon invasion and a health inspector
- Ragdoll physics on every customer, troublemaker and passer-by
- 4 PC tiers, 3 chair tiers, 12 upgrades and a VIP lounge expansion
- Daily goals, levels, 20 achievements and trash trick shots
- Progress saves automatically to your CrazyGames account

## Controls
Keyboard & mouse:
- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Shift: run
- Space: jump
- C: crouch
- Mouse: look around
- E: interact, pick up, give items (hold E to repair, mop, brew and unclog)
- Left mouse button: punch (hold for a MEGA punch)
- Right mouse button: throw the item you hold
- Q: drop item
- Tab: open the phone shop
- Esc: pause and settings

Mobile and tablet:
- Left thumb: floating joystick to move (push it all the way to run)
- Drag on the right side of the screen: look around
- Buttons: E (interact), punch (hold for a MEGA punch), throw, jump, sprint, phone shop and pause
